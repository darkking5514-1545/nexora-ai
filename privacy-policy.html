1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RH Studio PK ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Nexora AI Android application (the "App"), package name com.rhstudio.nexora. Nexora AI is a chat app that runs AI language models (in GGUF format) locally on your device. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Nexora AI does not require an account or login, so we do not collect any personal profile information from you directly. The only information collected is limited to what is described in the "Third-Party Services" and "Advertising" sections below, which is gathered by Google AdMob in connection with ads shown in the App.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your chat messages, prompts, or AI-generated responses
  • Your name, email address, or account credentials (no account is required)
  • Your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone data
  • Any usage analytics or behavioral tracking operated by us

Because chats are processed and stored entirely on your device, we have no server-side access to your conversations and cannot view, recover, or restore them.

4. On-Device Chat Storage

All prompts and AI-generated responses are processed locally on your device using the model files you load. Your chat history is stored only in the App's local storage on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You load your own model files into the App, and any licensing terms attached to those third-party models are your responsibility to review and follow.

5. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show a rewarded ad (when you tap "Watch Ad to Continue Chat" after reaching a message limit) and a small banner ad in the sidebar menu. AdMob may collect and process information such as your Advertising ID, IP address, and device/app information to serve and measure ads. This data is handled by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not control how Google processes this data.

6. Advertising and Advertising ID

Ads shown in the App may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on your device and account settings. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time from your Android device settings (typically under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google Settings → Ads).

7. Permissions Used

Nexora AI uses internet-related permissions solely to load and display ads through Google AdMob and to check network connectivity. These permissions are not used to transmit your chats, prompts, or model files to any server we operate.

8. Data Retention and How to Delete Your Data

Since all chat data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it. You can:

  • Delete individual chats or conversation history within the App at any time
  • Clear the App's data through your device's system Settings (Settings → Apps → Nexora AI → Storage → Clear Data)
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored chats and app data from your device

9. Data Security

Because chats and model data are stored locally, the overall security of that data depends on your device's security (for example, using a screen lock or device encryption). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and securing physical access to it.

10. Children's Privacy

Nexora AI is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address the matter.

11. User Rights and Choices

  • You may delete any or all chats within the App at any time.
  • You may uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data from your device.
  • You may control ad personalization and reset your Advertising ID through your device settings.
  • You may choose not to watch rewarded ads, subject to the App's message-limit functionality.

12. International Users

RH Studio PK is based in Pakistan. If you use the App from outside Pakistan, please note that any data collected by third-party services such as Google AdMob (as described above) may be processed in countries other than your own, in accordance with Google's own privacy practices.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable requirements. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
